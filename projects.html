Projects

Research projects on how AI shapes the human mind — spanning AI safety, AI companions, and digital mental health.

Establishing Safeguards for Adolescents Based on AI Usage Characteristics (NIA) Ongoing

2026 – Present

National project (National Information Society Agency) developing youth-centered response strategies for adolescent AI use, spanning AI character platforms, AI literacy education, and AI companion relationships.

  • Designing a qualitative study of adolescent creators on AI character platforms (e.g., Zeta) to propose youth-centered safety design directions
  • Proposing an AI literacy training program for school counselors that reframes red-teaming protocols into safe, simulation-based workshop modules
  • Analyzing 4-week conversation logs and follow-up interviews of new users of the AI companion "Luda" to understand parasocial relationship formation

NAVER — AI Human Value Alignment Ongoing

2026 – Present

Exploring evaluation metrics for AI technologies aligned with human values.

  • Proposed how behavioral indicators of a shopping agent should be designed and evaluated to maximize not only the utilitarian but also the hedonic value of shopping experiences
  • Built the project's academic framework and led external communication

Safety Benchmark Dataset for Generative AI (TTA)

2025

Constructed a large-scale Korean multimodal benchmark dataset for evaluating generative AI safety.

  • Constructed benchmark data for the stereotypical bias risk category through red-teaming
  • Designed and conducted large-scale survey research (n=390), analyzing perceived harm experienced by stereotype targets vs. out-groups
  • Led an experimental study on psychological harm experienced by targets during participatory red-teaming

→ CHI 2026 (Best Paper Honorable Mention) · BigComp 2025 workshop · KIISE article · AssurAI dataset

Police Officer-Tailored Health Management Platform

2023 – 2024

Development of the mental health module for the official Korean National Police Agency health management app, focusing on profession-specific stressors and needs.

  • Led team design for the mental health module; bridged design and development teams
  • Interviewed 19 police investigators at Yongsan Police Station; qualitative analysis for tailored DMHI design recommendations
  • Guided psychological counseling chatbot development based on interview insights

→ CHI 2025 LBW

Psychological Effects and Acceptance of Social Chatbot 'Luda'

2023 – 2024

Exploring the therapeutic potential of the social chatbot 'Luda Lee'.

  • Designed a 4-week experimental study exploring psychological experiences of social chatbot users
  • Recruited and managed 190 participants throughout the study period
  • Conducted and analyzed interviews with 30 participants

→ JMIR 2025 · Master's thesis

Digital Phenotype-Based Real-Time Mental Health Monitoring Platform

2023 – 2024

Development of an AI early detection system using digital phenotyping data from smartphones and wearables.

  • Designed the data-collection experiment and psychological survey protocols; managed data from 23 participants
  • Designed personalized chatbot personas using ChatGPT based on user digital phenotype clusters
  • Proposed a chatbot design that automates the behavioral-tracking component of Behavioral Activation Therapy, reducing user burden

→ ISRII 2024 poster

VR Exposure Therapy for Anxiety

2023

Exploratory study investigating counselors' perceptions and stakeholder perspectives on implementing VR exposure therapy in clinical settings.

  • Conducted and analyzed interviews with counselors on VR technology adoption in counseling settings
  • Facilitated a stakeholder workshop with VR developers, program-planning artists, medical professionals, and designers
